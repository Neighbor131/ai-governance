FAQ

Clear answers before you start.

Is this legal advice?

No. It is operational governance support. It can help legal teams move faster, but it does not replace legal counsel.

How long does a snapshot take?

Most snapshots take 1-2 weeks depending on access to product, vendor, and workflow information.

Can this support EU AI Act readiness?

Yes, by organizing use cases, evidence, risk ownership, literacy, and governance controls. Formal legal interpretation should sit with counsel.

Can this help with ISO readiness?

It can prepare operational materials and governance discipline that support readiness, but certification work should be handled by the relevant audit specialists.

Do you work with early-stage teams?

Yes. The system scales from lightweight startup governance to more formal enterprise-facing evidence packs.
